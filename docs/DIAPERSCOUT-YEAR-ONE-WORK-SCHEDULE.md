# DiaperScout Year One Work Schedule

**Status:** Proposed working schedule; revise after the first month of evidence.

**Capacity:** 2–3 hours a week, including planning, review, catalogue work and maintenance.

This schedule puts the [Year One experiment](DIAPERSCOUT-YEAR-ONE-EXPERIMENT.md) into practice. October has concrete work sessions; later periods have reviewable themes rather than fixed feature deadlines. It does not assert that planned capabilities are already live or authorise infrastructure spending.

## Weekly rhythm

Book two one-hour sessions on days that suit the week. No particular evening is required.

- **Session A:** 10 minutes to check costs, service health and the previous action; 50 minutes on the current priority.
- **Session B:** 40 minutes to finish or review that priority; 15 minutes of catalogue verification; 5 minutes to record the result and next action.
- **Optional third hour:** finish a bounded task, resolve an operational issue or improve the Guide's personality/mobile experience. This is optional capacity, not hidden delivery commitment.

Keep one main task in progress. Count briefing AI, reviewing changes, checking behaviour and deployment decisions as work. AI execution time need not consume the whole allowance, but its outputs still need review. If a task exceeds its session, shrink it or carry it forward; do not borrow automatically from the following evening. Missed weeks shift the queue rather than create catch-up debt.

At two hours per week, expect roughly eight core hours in a typical four-week month. A service or recovery problem takes priority and displaces planned feature work. Each monthly review replaces a normal session rather than adding another meeting.

## Compact scorecard: proposed definitions

Use a simple monthly record initially. Record the reporting period, product/revenue launch dates, instrumentation coverage and known exclusions alongside the figures. Establish baselines before setting growth targets. These definitions are a planning proposal to confirm in the first session, not an instruction to add every possible metric.

| Question | Core measure and definition | Initial source / limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Are people reaching the Guide? | Monthly unique visitors, using the selected analytics tool's documented visitor definition. | Privacy-conscious analytics; record coverage and exclusions. Do not equate devices or identifiers with exact people. |
| Does search lead somewhere useful? | Search-to-product engagement: measured search journeys with at least one resulting product open / measured search journeys × 100. A journey begins with a submitted search and ends at the next submitted search or session end. | Minimal search/result-open events. This is a proxy for usefulness, not proof of satisfaction. Zero-result searches are a diagnostic; they do not capture every unhelpful result. |
| Do product pages help people reach a retailer? | Outbound retailer CTR: measured product-page views generating at least one retailer click / measured product-page views × 100. Count a view once in the numerator; distinguish affiliate clicks from other retailer clicks. | Product-view and outbound-click events linked within the current page context. Affiliate click totals are retained separately for commercial reporting. |
| Is the product covering its costs? | Monthly revenue, monthly recurring operating cost and cost coverage = revenue / operating cost × 100. | Expense record plus affiliate/other revenue statements. Use approved/earned revenue for coverage; show provisional reported revenue and cash received separately. Allocate annual recurring fees across their covered months and retain actual payment dates in the cash record. |
| Is community contribution developing? | Monthly active contributors: distinct non-internal Explorers making at least one submitted contribution during the month. Show accepted contributions and open moderation backlog alongside it. | Submission/moderation records once public contribution is live. Activity is not the same as acceptance or quality. Before launch, mark unavailable rather than zero demand. |

For the same reporting period, retain separate totals for AI/development spend and cumulative personal cash invested. Record personal funds paid into or directly spent on the project once, without double-counting transfers and their subsequent expenditure. Record any reimbursements separately so gross personal funding remains visible.

Initially collect operating costs and verified provider reports manually if that is sufficient. Tool selection must fit the budget and privacy principles; this schedule does not prescribe a paid analytics service. Keep internal/test activity excluded or labelled where practical, and describe residual uncertainty. Use N/A for missing instrumentation or zero denominators, not a fabricated zero percentage. Never fingerprint people to obtain a returning-visitor count.

Acquisition source, returning visitors, product views, searches, registrations and Where-to-Buy usage can provide diagnostic context when available. Approval rate, if used, is approved / (approved + rejected) decisions in the period, excluding pending work and with repeated decisions disclosed. Do not confuse this with eventual acceptance of a submission cohort.

Once an affiliate mechanism is live, report tracked affiliate clicks and qualifying purchases alongside attributable revenue. Attributed conversion is reported qualifying purchases / tracked affiliate clicks × 100; earnings per click and revenue per product view can follow when denominators and reporting periods are reliable. Record attribution windows, reporting delays and eligibility rules; research programme specifics before comparisons. No qualifying test purchases are required to validate click instrumentation.

## October 2026: establish a measurable, useful path

The dates reserve attention, not guaranteed delivery. A week is successful when it produces a checked result or a clearly bounded next task. Existing Product Manager, Retail Manager and affiliate-research foundations should be inspected and reused before new work is commissioned.

| Window | Session A | Session B | Evidence / completion condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1–7 October** | Confirm the scorecard definitions; list actual recurring costs, AI/development spend and personal funding; set an affordable spending ceiling. | Check the current public discovery journey, analytics availability and evidence of backup/recovery readiness. Record what is working, missing or unverified. | A small baseline record with sources and gaps, plus one highest-priority implementation task. Unknown production readiness remains explicit. |
| **8–14 October** | Walk one real product through **Add business → Add listing → Where to Buy** using the existing tools; identify the smallest missing or broken step. | Complete/review that step if small enough, or prepare one bounded fix. Verify a small catalogue sample and its destination/rights evidence. | One checked end-to-end manual example, or a reproducible blocker with a scoped fix. No requirement to clear all imported candidates. |
| **15–21 October** | Review existing affiliate research for a useful retailer; investigate eligibility, permitted linking, reporting and costs. Record a candidate and next action rather than assuming Amazon/eBay priority. | Add or verify the minimum outbound measurement for the manual journey, subject to review and normal release checks. If already present, reconcile a controlled test event with the report. | A programme decision/research record and a demonstrated tracking path, or explicit blockers. If an approved mechanism is ready sooner, begin commercial validation sooner. |
| **22–28 October** | Progress the first eligible tracked revenue mechanism, if access and tracking are ready. If waiting on approval, improve useful direct listings and basic discoverability instead. | Review the mobile discovery journey and choose one small welcome/Guide-personality improvement. Use remaining time for catalogue quality. | A live, checked commercial path where feasible; otherwise a named external dependency and useful work completed meanwhile. One bounded identity improvement, not a home-page rebuild. |
| **29–31 October** | Use the first hour for the month-end review: what shipped, what visitors could actually use, costs, measurement gaps and capacity. | Reserve the second hour for carry-over or rest; select November's first task only after reviewing the evidence. | October baseline, dates when measurement/revenue became live, and a short November queue. This partial week adds no obligation to find extra evenings. |

Commercial launch depends on programme access, useful destinations, working attribution and production readiness. Do not promise revenue or programme approval on a calendar date. A plugin is unnecessary if a permitted manual affiliate destination can test the hypothesis. Start outreach once the relevant public journey is usable and measurement is adequate; do not wait for a plugin or the Full Vision. Outreach itself remains a separately authorised activity.

## Rolling direction after October

At each monthly review, choose the next small outcome from this queue and reassess estimates. Move unfinished foundations forward before adding commitments. The periods below describe intended emphasis, not promises to ship every named capability.

| Period | Intended emphasis | Decision before expanding scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **November–December 2026** | Improve catalogue coverage/quality, useful retailer destinations, tracking and discoverability; begin targeted outreach and learn from real use. Advance business bank account and required business/affiliate administration; research registration and trademark questions. Include small mobile/identity improvements. | Can people complete the core journey, and can results be measured? Is a plugin solving a demonstrated limitation of manual listings? External deadlines or actual legal requirements take precedence over this provisional placement. |
| **January–March 2027** | Continue commercial learning and consider one small contribution loop through the existing submission/moderation model. Address governance and capacity before widening participation. Consider PWA improvements where observed mobile use supports them. | Is the core service maintainable within the allowance? Can incoming contributions be attributed, reviewed, corrected and moderated without creating an unmanaged backlog? |
| **April–June 2027** | Depending on evidence, deepen retailer support or develop a bounded Full Vision slice: barcode-assisted submission, local observations, Backpack history, or Atlas map exploration. Choose one, not all. | Are dependencies ready and does this respond to useful evidence or a clear differentiation objective? Local observations precede a map that depends on those observations; facility data needs its own source/permission checks. |
| **July–September 2027** | Give shipped work time in-market, improve what is useful, close measurement gaps and prepare the Year One review. Continue small worthwhile improvements without turning September into a feature-completion deadline. | What do trajectory, community value, actual exposure time, cost and available capacity support next? Review on 30 September using the strategy's unranked outcomes. |

Native iPhone/iOS work remains outside the scheduled commitments. Revisit only when mobile web/PWA use and maintenance capacity justify it. Retailer/manufacturer permissions and relationships progress as needed for the products and destinations being worked on, rather than waiting for a separate campaign.

## Weekly record and monthly review

Keep a short record with: date; time spent; task; checked outcome; evidence or link; blocker; next action. Record maintenance and catalogue work as real work. Do not fill the record with raw personal information or production credentials.

At month end, answer:

1. What was actually available to Explorers, and for how long?
2. What does the compact scorecard show, and what is still unknown?
3. What did it cost in operating spend, development spend, personal cash and time?
4. What should continue, change, wait or stop?
5. What is the next single priority, and what small piece of identity/community value belongs alongside it?

Keep future months adjustable. The purpose of the schedule is to make progress possible within 2–3 hours a week, not to turn the Year One inventory into an accumulating list of overdue promises.
