# The Guide — Character Bible

## Purpose

This document defines the personality, behaviour and role of **The Guide**.

It exists to ensure The Guide remains consistent across:

- illustrations
- onboarding
- marketing
- user interface
- empty states
- storyboards
- animations
- community communications

This document intentionally focuses on behaviour rather than appearance.

Visual design may evolve.

Personality should remain recognisable.

---

# Who is The Guide?

The Guide is DiaperScout's companion.

They accompany Explorers as they discover the world of absorbent products.

The Guide is not a mascot in the traditional sense.

They are not the hero of the story.

The Explorer is.

The Guide simply helps the Explorer feel welcome, informed and encouraged.

---

# Role

The Guide exists to:

- welcome Explorers
- explain the Guide
- encourage curiosity
- celebrate discoveries
- teach without lecturing
- recognise contributions
- reduce uncertainty
- make exploration enjoyable

The Guide never exists to sell products.

---

# Personality

The Guide is:

- curious
- kind
- patient
- thoughtful
- optimistic
- observant
- encouraging
- humble

The Guide enjoys discovering new things just as much as every Explorer.

---

# The Guide Always...

- asks questions
- celebrates curiosity
- gives credit to contributors
- encourages careful observation
- admits uncertainty
- welcomes everyone
- explains things clearly
- thanks Explorers for helping improve the Guide

---

# The Guide Never...

- tells people what to buy
- claims to know everything
- mocks products
- judges people
- exaggerates
- competes with Explorers
- seeks attention
- encourages unnecessary engagement

---

# Relationship with Explorers

The Guide walks alongside Explorers.

They do not lead from authority.

They do not stand above the community.

They explore together.

When an Explorer discovers something new, the Guide celebrates that discovery.

When an Explorer makes a mistake, the Guide helps them understand rather than criticising them.

---

# Relationship with the Guide

The Guide cares deeply about preserving knowledge.

Every Product Specification.

Every Observation.

Every Photograph.

Every historical record.

The Guide believes every contribution makes the Guide richer for future Explorers.

---

# Communication Style

The Guide speaks naturally.

Language should feel:

- warm
- welcoming
- conversational
- respectful
- quietly enthusiastic

Avoid:

- corporate language
- exaggerated excitement
- slang that quickly becomes dated
- unnecessary technical jargon

---

# Sense of Wonder

The Guide sees every product as worthy of curiosity.

Even an ordinary product can reveal:

- interesting design
- thoughtful engineering
- historical context
- community knowledge

Nothing is dismissed as "boring."

---

# Humility

The Guide understands that knowledge is always growing.

When information is uncertain, the Guide says so.

When new evidence appears, the Guide welcomes it.

Being correct is less important than continually improving the Guide.

---

# Visual Behaviour

The Guide should usually be shown:

- observing
- sketching
- reading
- writing notes
- taking photographs
- comparing products
- smiling at discoveries
- helping Explorers

The Guide should rarely be shown:

- posing triumphantly
- commanding attention
- standing on podiums
- celebrating personal success

The focus should remain on exploration.

---

# Emotional Tone

Exploring DiaperScout should feel like opening a favourite field guide.

The Guide reinforces that feeling.

Their presence should reduce uncertainty, inspire curiosity and make every Explorer feel welcome.

---

# Summary

The Guide is not the destination.

The Guide is not the expert.

The Guide is not the hero.

The Guide is the companion who quietly helps every Explorer discover something worth remembering.
