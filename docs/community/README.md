# The Guide and its Scouts

DiaperScout is an explorer's guide.

No single person can document every product, every retailer, every country or every packaging revision.

The Guide exists because thousands of individual discoveries, made by Scouts around the world, are brought together into one shared body of knowledge.

Every observation helps the Guide understand the world a little better.

Every Explorer benefits from those who came before them.

---

# Explorers

Everyone who uses DiaperScout is an Explorer.

Explorers come to the Guide with questions.

- What products exist?
- Where can I buy them?
- Has anyone used them?
- Are they available near me?
- How do they compare?

The Guide exists to help answer those questions.

Explorers do not need to contribute.

Simply using the Guide is enough.

---

# Scouts

Some Explorers choose to help the Guide grow.

When an Explorer's first accepted contribution becomes part of the Guide, they become a Scout.

A Scout is an Explorer who has chosen to bring knowledge back to the Guide.

Scouts document what they discover.

They record observations.

They preserve knowledge.

Every discovery they share helps the Guide become more complete.

---

# The Guide Grows Through Discovery

The Guide cannot explore the world on its own.

Every supermarket.

Every pharmacy.

Every retailer.

Every country.

Every packaging revision.

Every discontinued product.

Every historical catalogue.

These become part of the Guide because Scouts encounter them and choose to document them.

Some discoveries happen during everyday shopping.

Others happen while travelling.

Others come from careful research.

Every genuine discovery helps the Guide become more complete.

---

# Observations Become Evidence

The Guide is not built from opinions.

It is built from observations.

Every observation represents one person's experience at one place and one moment in time.

Individually those observations tell small stories.

Together they become evidence.

As evidence grows, so does the Guide's understanding of the world.

---

# Facts and Evidence

DiaperScout deliberately separates facts from evidence.

Facts describe products.

Observations describe experiences.

Facts come from authoritative sources, typically manufacturers.

Observations come from Scouts.

Neither replaces the other.

Together they help Explorers make informed decisions.

---

# Trust

Trust is earned.

Trust does not determine truth.

Trust adds weight to evidence.

Its purpose is not to create status.

Its purpose is to help the Guide better understand the world.

Trust exists within the Guide.

It quietly improves the quality of the evidence presented to Explorers.

---

# The Guide Comes First

The Guide is always the main character.

Every community feature exists for one reason:

To help Explorers discover more of the world.

Scout profiles exist to provide context for contributions.

Backpacks celebrate journeys.

Pins recognise meaningful contributions.

Recognition exists to celebrate stewardship rather than competition.

If a feature does not make the Guide better, it does not belong in DiaperScout.

---

# Quality Over Quantity

The Guide values careful observation more than volume.

One well documented discovery may be more valuable than hundreds of routine observations.

Scouts are recognised for the quality, diversity and care of their contributions.

The community celebrates contribution rather than competition.

---

# Evidence Over Opinion

Evidence is challenged by more evidence.

Not by popularity.

Not by votes.

Not by debate.

The Guide grows stronger through continued observation.

As new evidence is gathered, the Guide's understanding continues to evolve.

---

# Shared Stewardship

The Guide is built from many sources of knowledge.

Manufacturers contribute facts.

Automation gathers information that computers can reliably discover.

Scouts document the world around them.

Editors preserve the integrity of the Guide.

Each source contributes what it knows best.

Together they create something that none could build alone.

---

# Our Purpose

Every Explorer begins with curiosity.

Every Scout returns with knowledge.

Together they make the Guide possible.
