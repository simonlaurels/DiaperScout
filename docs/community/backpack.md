# Backpack

## Purpose

Every Scout has a Backpack.

The Backpack is the Scout's public identity within DiaperScout.

It records the Scout's journey, celebrates meaningful contribution and provides provenance for the knowledge they have added to the Guide.

The Backpack exists to support the Guide.

It is not intended to function as a social media profile.

---

# Design Principles

The Backpack should:

- celebrate contribution
- encourage exploration
- provide provenance
- remain personal
- avoid competition

The Backpack should never encourage popularity, engagement farming or social networking.

---

# Identity

Every Backpack belongs to a single Scout.

A Backpack is identified by:

- Scout Callsign
- Join date
- Optional biography
- Optional home region
- Optional avatar

Real names are never required.

---

# Scout Pins

Scout Pins are displayed on the Backpack.

Pins represent meaningful contribution rather than achievement.

Scouts choose which Pins to display.

The Backpack should display only a small number of featured Pins.

This allows every Backpack to tell a different story.

---

# Field Journal

Every Backpack contains a Field Journal.

The Field Journal is a chronological record of the Scout's accepted discoveries.

Examples include:

- product discoveries
- retailer observations
- photographs
- measurements
- historical research

Every entry links back to the corresponding observation.

The Field Journal provides provenance for the Guide.

---

# Discoveries

The Backpack should celebrate discoveries.

Notable discoveries may be highlighted.

Examples include:

- first product documented
- first country explored
- significant historical discovery
- major contribution to a product

These highlights help tell the Scout's story.

---

# Contribution Summary

The Backpack may summarise contribution.

Examples include:

- products documented
- retailers visited
- countries explored
- photographs contributed
- measurements recorded

These summaries exist to provide context rather than comparison.

---

# Impact

Occasionally the Guide may reflect on the impact of a Scout's work.

Examples include:

- Explorers helped
- products improved
- observations contributing to evidence

Impact summaries should remain infrequent.

Their purpose is reflection rather than engagement.

---

# Privacy

Scouts control how much of their Backpack is public.

Examples include:

- biography
- avatar
- home region

Contribution history remains public because it provides provenance for the Guide.

Internal trust is never displayed.

---

# Deliberately Excluded

The Backpack intentionally excludes:

- follower counts
- following
- direct messaging
- comments
- likes
- popularity rankings
- public trust scores
- leaderboards

These concepts do not contribute to the Guide.

---

# Relationship with the Guide

The Backpack exists because the Guide values provenance.

Every discovery has an author.

Every observation has a history.

The Backpack allows Explorers to understand the journey behind those discoveries without distracting from the Guide itself.

---

# Summary

Every Backpack tells a story.

Not the story of popularity.

The story of curiosity.

Every Pin.

Every observation.

Every discovery.

Together they describe one Scout's journey through the world.
