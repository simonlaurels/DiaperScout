# Community Contributions

## Purpose

DiaperScout is strengthened by Explorers who choose to share what they discover.

Contributions bring knowledge back from the world so that future Explorers can discover products, locations and experiences that might otherwise remain undocumented.

Community contributions are a primary source of knowledge within DiaperScout.

Contributors help the Guide understand the world.

They do not define the Guide.

---

# Becoming a Contributor

Every account begins as an Explorer.

Explorers may use the Guide without ever contributing.

When an Explorer contributes information to DiaperScout, they become a **Contributor**.

Contributor is not a rank, reward or account level.

It simply describes an Explorer who has chosen to contribute knowledge to the Guide.

An Explorer remains an Explorer whether or not they contribute.

---

# Public Identity

Explorers may choose a public display name for their contributions.

Real names are not required.

Contributions are attributed to the Explorer's chosen public identity rather than requiring their real-world identity to be displayed.

A good display name should be:

* unique
* memorable
* respectful

The public identity exists to provide attribution while protecting privacy.

---

# The Backpack

Every Explorer has a Backpack.

The Backpack is the personal space within DiaperScout that records an Explorer's journey.

Unlike a traditional social media profile, the Backpack exists to tell the story of exploration rather than measure popularity.

A Backpack may contain:

* Explorer display name
* Scrapbook
* notable discoveries
* Discovery Journal
* contribution highlights
* saved products and locations
* personal collections
* occasional impact summaries

The Backpack intentionally avoids:

* follower counts
* popularity metrics
* public trust scores
* engagement statistics
* leaderboards

The Backpack belongs to the Explorer.

The Guide remains the main character.

---

# The Discovery Journal

Accepted observations and other meaningful contributions form part of an Explorer's Discovery Journal.

The Discovery Journal is a chronological record of discoveries and contributions.

It provides provenance for community knowledge while allowing Explorers to understand their own journey through the world.

A Discovery Journal may contain:

* product discoveries
* location observations
* measurements
* photographs
* historical research
* other accepted Community Observations

Contributions remain attributed to the Explorer who recorded them.

Where corrections are necessary, they are made transparently.

The history of significant observations is preserved.

---

# The Scrapbook

The Scrapbook recognises meaningful contribution and memorable discoveries.

It does not represent rank.

It does not represent authority.

It does not create competition.

Instead, it tells the story of an Explorer's journey and the ways they have helped the Guide.

Scrapbook items may recognise contributions such as:

* discovering new products
* documenting locations
* measuring products
* photographing products
* preserving historical information
* expanding the Guide into new places
* making particularly useful discoveries

Recognition should feel like collecting memories from an expedition rather than accumulating points.

Explorers may choose how their Scrapbook is presented.

No two Scrapbooks need look the same.

---

# Recognition

Recognition celebrates contribution.

It does not reward activity for its own sake.

DiaperScout values:

* curiosity
* careful observation
* thoughtful documentation
* stewardship
* long-term contribution

A single important discovery may be more valuable than hundreds of routine observations.

Recognition exists to celebrate an Explorer's contribution to the Guide rather than encourage competition between Explorers.

---

# Impact

Occasionally, the Guide may reflect on how an Explorer's discoveries have helped others.

These summaries exist to acknowledge meaningful contribution rather than measure popularity.

Examples include:

* Explorers helped
* products documented
* locations documented
* countries explored
* observations contributing to community knowledge

Impact summaries should be infrequent.

Their purpose is reflection rather than engagement.

---

# Privacy

DiaperScout respects the privacy of every Explorer.

Real names are never required for public contribution.

Public display names form the visible identity of community contributions.

Internal trust and moderation information remains private.

Explorers should never feel that contributing knowledge requires revealing more personal information than necessary.

---

# Community

Contributors are collaborators.

Not competitors.

When Explorers encounter conflicting information, they gather more evidence.

Evidence becomes stronger through continued discovery rather than debate.

Every Explorer contributes a different piece of the world.

Together those discoveries allow the Guide to become richer, broader and more useful than any individual could create alone.

---

# Summary

Every Explorer begins with curiosity.

Some choose to contribute.

Contributors discover.

Contributors document.

Contributors help preserve knowledge.

Every meaningful discovery can become part of the Guide.

One observation.

One location.

One product.

One journey.

At a time.
