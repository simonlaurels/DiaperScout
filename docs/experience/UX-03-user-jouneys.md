**Phase:** Experience Design  
**Status:** Draft

# Purpose

This document defines the experiences people have while using DiaperScout.

Unlike technical documentation, these journeys focus on the human experience rather than the interface itself.

Every journey describes:

- Why someone opened DiaperScout.
- What they hope to achieve.
- How the experience unfolds.
- How they should feel throughout the journey.
- What naturally happens next.

The objective is not simply to complete tasks.

The objective is to create memorable moments of discovery.

---

# Journey Design Philosophy

Every journey should feel:

- Natural
- Calm
- Encouraging
- Curious
- Rewarding

Visitors should rarely encounter dead ends.

Whenever possible, every journey should naturally encourage another.

Rather than asking:

> "What screen comes next?"

DiaperScout asks:

> "What would someone naturally want to do next?"

# Journey One

# A New World

## Purpose

Introduce visitors to DiaperScout.

Not by teaching features.

By inviting them into a living world.

---

## Starting Context

Someone has just installed DiaperScout.

They may know very little about absorbent products.

They may simply be curious.

No account is required.

No commitment has been made.

---

## Emotional Journey

Curiosity

↓

Welcome

↓

Wonder

↓

Exploration

---

## Experience

The application opens quietly.

Rather than presenting forms or requests for information, visitors are welcomed by **The Guide**.

The Guide briefly introduces the world of DiaperScout.

Through a series of short illustrated scenes, visitors discover that DiaperScout is a community-built atlas of products and places.

The Guide introduces the five destinations:

- 🌿 Explore
- 📦 Products
- 📷 Scan
- 🗺️ Atlas
- 🎒 Backpack

The introduction remains brief, visual and optional.

Visitors may skip it immediately.

Its purpose is not to explain every feature.

Its purpose is simply to spark curiosity.

The final illustration shows the world waiting to be explored.

The Guide smiles.

> "Ready to explore?"

The visitor enters DiaperScout.

---

## Outcome

The visitor understands the purpose of the platform without feeling overwhelmed.

The application feels welcoming rather than instructional.

---

## Natural Next Step

Explore

---

# Journey Two

# Finding Something Familiar

## Purpose

Help someone find information about a known product.

---

## Starting Context

The visitor already knows what they are looking for.

Perhaps they know:

- the product name
- the brand
- the product type

---

## Emotional Journey

Confidence

↓

Discovery

↓

Understanding

---

## Experience

The visitor browses the Products section.

Search and filters make finding products effortless.

Upon opening a product, they discover much more than basic information.

Photographs.

Historical packaging.

Reviews.

Community observations.

Where the product has been discovered.

Rather than simply answering a question, the product page invites further exploration.

---

## Outcome

The visitor understands the product more deeply than expected.

---

## Natural Next Step

Explore related products.

Compare products.

View observations.

Visit the Atlas.

---

# Journey Three

# An Unexpected Discovery

## Purpose

Transform an unsuccessful search into the beginning of a new story.

---

## Starting Context

The visitor has discovered a product that may not yet exist within DiaperScout.

They simply want to identify it.

---

## Emotional Journey

Curiosity

↓

Surprise

↓

Excitement

↓

Possibility

---

## Experience

The visitor scans the barcode.

The Guide quietly waits while DiaperScout searches the Atlas.

No matching product is found.

Rather than presenting an error, the application celebrates the moment.

The visitor learns that nobody has documented this product yet.

The Guide gently explains that every discovery begins somewhere.

The visitor is invited to help document the product for everyone.

No pressure.

Just an invitation.

---

## Outcome

A failed lookup becomes an opportunity to expand the Atlas.

---

## Natural Next Step

Help document the product.

---

# Journey Four

# Helping the Atlas

## Purpose

Guide someone's first contribution.

---

## Starting Context

The visitor has decided to help.

This is their first contribution.

---

## Emotional Journey

Encouragement

↓

Purpose

↓

Pride

↓

Belonging

---

## Experience

The Guide explains why observations matter.

The visitor is guided through documenting the product.

Photos.

Packaging.

Location.

Evidence.

Each step reinforces that they are helping future visitors rather than completing a form.

Once finished, the Atlas has grown.

The contribution becomes part of the shared knowledge of the community.

The Guide thanks the visitor.

---

## Outcome

The visitor feels that their effort genuinely mattered.

---

## Natural Next Step

Continue exploring.

---

# Journey Five

# Exploring Places

## Purpose

Encourage geographical exploration.

---

## Starting Context

The visitor wants to discover places rather than products.

Perhaps they are planning a shopping trip.

Perhaps they simply enjoy exploring.

---

## Emotional Journey

Curiosity

↓

Exploration

↓

Discovery

---

## Experience

Atlas reveals the places where products have genuinely been observed.

Maps.

Lists.

Nearby discoveries.

Online retailers.

Every location tells its own story through community observations.

Visitors quickly realise the Atlas is built from evidence rather than assumptions.

---

## Outcome

The visitor discovers places worth exploring.

---

## Natural Next Step

Visit a product.

Explore another place.

Help improve the Atlas.

---

# Journey Six

# Returning

## Purpose

Reward returning visitors.

---

## Starting Context

The visitor has not opened DiaperScout for some time.

---

## Emotional Journey

Curiosity

↓

Recognition

↓

Connection

---

## Experience

Explore feels alive.

New discoveries.

Community observations.

Atlas growth.

Interesting products.

The world has changed since the visitor was last here.

The Guide quietly highlights something interesting rather than demanding attention.

The visitor immediately has something worth exploring.

---

## Outcome

Returning feels rewarding.

The world continues to grow even while the visitor is away.

---

## Natural Next Step

Any journey.


Visitors should rarely reach a dead end.

---

# Summary

DiaperScout is designed around moments rather than tasks.

Every journey should create one or more lasting feelings:

- Curiosity
- Wonder
- Confidence
- Encouragement
- Pride
- Belonging

By consistently reinforcing these emotions, DiaperScout becomes more than a searchable catalogue.

It becomes a living world that people genuinely enjoy exploring.
