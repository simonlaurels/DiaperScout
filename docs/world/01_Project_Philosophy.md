# Project Philosophy

## Purpose

DiaperScout exists to celebrate, document, and explore the world of absorbent products.

It is not a traditional shopping app.

It is not a review website.

It is not an affiliate marketing platform.

It is an explorer's guide.

---

## Our Mission

To build the world's most complete, welcoming, and trustworthy guide to absorbent products.

Every product has a story.

Every product deserves to be documented.

Every user deserves accurate information presented without judgement.

---

## Our Belief

Knowledge removes uncertainty.

Choosing an absorbent product should feel like preparing for an adventure, not navigating a confusing catalogue.

People should be able to understand what a product is, how it behaves, where it excels, and where it may not be the right choice.

DiaperScout exists to provide that understanding.

---

## Discovery First

Discovery is more important than shopping.

Users come to DiaperScout to learn.

Purchasing may happen afterwards, but it is never the primary purpose of the platform.

Curiosity always comes first.

---

## Community Knowledge

The best information comes from people with real experience.

DiaperScout encourages community knowledge while recognising that personal experiences vary.

Individual opinions are valuable, but evidence and collective understanding build trust.

---

## Respect

DiaperScout welcomes everyone with an interest in absorbent products.

This includes, but is not limited to:

- Parents and caregivers
- Healthcare professionals
- Adults managing continence needs
- People with disabilities
- Enthusiasts and collectors
- Members of the ABDL community

No legitimate reason for using or appreciating absorbent products is treated as more valid than another.

Respect is fundamental.

---

## Tone

DiaperScout should feel:

- Curious
- Friendly
- Knowledgeable
- Honest
- Playful where appropriate
- Respectful at all times

The product should never mock, shame, sensationalise, or judge.

---

## Long-Term Vision

DiaperScout aims to become the definitive explorer's guide to absorbent products worldwide.

Every new feature, article, image, and line of copy should move the project closer to that goal.

---

## Guiding Question

When making a decision, ask:

> Does this help people discover and understand absorbent products better?

If the answer is yes, it is probably aligned with the philosophy of DiaperScout.
