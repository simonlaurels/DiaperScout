# Core Principles

These principles define how DiaperScout should evolve.

Every feature, design decision, piece of copy, and technical implementation should support these principles whenever practical.

---

# 1. Discovery Before Shopping

DiaperScout exists to help people discover and understand products.

Commercial features must never undermine exploration or learning.

---

# 2. Accuracy Matters

Information should be as accurate, complete, and verifiable as possible.

Where uncertainty exists, it should be acknowledged rather than hidden.

Trust is earned through honesty.

---

# 3. Community Makes the Guide Better

The community is one of DiaperScout's greatest strengths.

Users should be encouraged to contribute knowledge, corrections, experiences, photographs, and discoveries.

Community contributions should improve the guide over time.

---

# 4. Respect Every User

People use absorbent products for many different reasons.

DiaperScout welcomes everyone equally.

No assumptions should be made about why someone is interested in a product.

---

# 5. Respect Every Product

Every product deserves documentation.

Whether a product is premium, budget, discontinued, niche, medical, or widely available, it is part of the history of absorbent products.

---

# 6. Celebrate Curiosity

Exploration should feel rewarding.

Users should feel encouraged to browse, compare, discover unusual products, and learn something new.

Curiosity is a feature, not a distraction.

---

# 7. Evidence Over Opinion

Personal experiences are valuable.

Objective information is essential.

Where possible, factual measurements, specifications, and documented characteristics should be distinguished from subjective experiences.

---

# 8. Be Honest About Limitations

No guide can know everything.

If information is incomplete, estimated, or community-sourced, that should be clearly indicated.

Transparency builds confidence.

---

# 9. Consistency Builds Trust

The same product should be described consistently throughout the application.

Terminology, measurements, ratings, and classifications should follow shared standards.

---

# 10. Build for the Long Term

DiaperScout is intended to become a lasting reference.

Short-term convenience should not compromise long-term quality, maintainability, or trustworthiness.

---

# Decision Filter

Before introducing a feature, ask:

- Does it improve discovery?
- Does it improve understanding?
- Does it increase trust?
- Does it respect every user?
- Will it still make sense years from now?

If most answers are "yes", the feature is probably aligned with DiaperScout's principles.
