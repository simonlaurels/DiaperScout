# World Rules

These rules define the DiaperScout universe.

They are not technical constraints. They are creative and editorial guidelines that help every part of the project feel consistent.

---

# The World Is Built Around Exploration

DiaperScout is an explorer's guide.

Users are explorers.

Products are discoveries.

Knowledge is the reward for exploration.

The experience should encourage curiosity rather than consumption.

---

# Every Product Has Value

Every absorbent product deserves documentation.

No product is considered too ordinary, too old, too niche, too inexpensive, or too unusual to be included.

If a product exists, it is part of the world.

---

# Information Comes Before Opinion

Facts should always be presented before subjective experiences.

Whenever possible, objective information should be separated from personal impressions.

Users should be able to distinguish between:

- Manufacturer specifications
- Measured data
- Community observations
- Individual experiences

---

# Discovery Should Feel Rewarding

Finding a previously unseen product should feel exciting.

Learning something unexpected should feel satisfying.

The platform should encourage exploration without manipulating users through artificial scarcity or pressure.

---

# Curiosity Is Always Welcome

There is no "correct" reason to be interested in absorbent products.

People arrive with different needs, experiences, and interests.

DiaperScout welcomes curiosity in all its forms, provided interactions remain respectful.

---

# Brands Are Part of the Landscape

Manufacturers create products.

DiaperScout documents them.

The platform is independent.

It exists to inform rather than promote.

---

# The Guide Is Never Finished

DiaperScout is intended to grow continuously.

New products appear.

Old products disappear.

Packaging changes.

Communities discover new information.

The guide should always be considered a living document.

---

# Mistakes Are Opportunities

Errors will happen.

When new information becomes available, the guide should be updated.

Improving knowledge is more important than defending previous assumptions.

---

# Respect Is Non-Negotiable

Every user should feel welcome.

Every contributor should be treated with respect.

Every discussion should assume good faith unless proven otherwise.

---

# Canon Evolves Carefully

Major changes to terminology, philosophy, or the world should be recorded in the project's canon.

This ensures that DiaperScout develops intentionally rather than accidentally.
