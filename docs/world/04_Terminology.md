# Terminology

This document defines the canonical language used throughout the DiaperScout project.

Documentation, user interface text, marketing material, and technical documentation should use these terms consistently wherever practical.

---

# Explorer

An **Explorer** is any person using DiaperScout.

The term is intentionally inclusive and makes no assumptions about why someone is using absorbent products.

---

# Discovery

A **Discovery** is the act of finding a product, learning new information, or exploring the database.

Discovery is one of the core experiences of DiaperScout.

---

# Guide

The **Guide** is DiaperScout itself.

Rather than presenting itself as a shop or catalogue, DiaperScout acts as an explorer's guide to absorbent products.

---

# Product

A **Product** is a specific absorbent product produced by a manufacturer.

Each product has its own record within the guide.

---

# Product Entry

A **Product Entry** is the complete collection of information about a product within DiaperScout.

A Product Entry may include:

- Manufacturer information
- Brand
- Product name
- Barcodes
- Specifications
- Measurements
- Packaging
- Photographs
- Availability
- Community observations
- Historical information

---

# Community Observation

A **Community Observation** is information contributed by Explorers based on their own experience.

Observations complement factual data but do not replace it.

Observations are intended to help others understand a product rather than simply judge it.

---

# Verified Data

**Verified Data** is information that has been confirmed through reliable evidence.

Examples include:

- Manufacturer specifications
- Measured dimensions
- Barcode verification
- Packaging details
- Official documentation
- Verified photographs

---

# Canon

**Canon** refers to officially accepted information about the DiaperScout project itself.

When documentation conflicts, the latest approved canonical documentation takes precedence.

---

# Explorer's Guide

**Explorer's Guide** is the preferred description of DiaperScout.

Avoid describing the project primarily as:

- A review site
- A shopping app
- A catalogue
- A database

While DiaperScout contains elements of all of these, none of them fully describe its purpose.

---

# Explore

**Explore** is the preferred action word throughout the project.

Examples include:

- Explore products
- Explore brands
- Explore discoveries
- Explore collections

Where appropriate, prefer "Explore" over:

- Browse
- Shop
- Purchase

---

# Collection

A **Collection** is a curated group of products brought together for a shared purpose.

Examples include:

- New discoveries
- Vintage products
- Products from a single manufacturer
- Community favourites

---

# Manufacturer

A **Manufacturer** is the company responsible for producing a product.

Manufacturers may own one or more brands.

---

# Brand

A **Brand** is a named product range or identity owned by a manufacturer.

A brand may contain multiple individual products.

---

# Respectful Language

The project should avoid language that assumes:

- Why someone uses a product
- Their age
- Their medical circumstances
- Their lifestyle
- Their identity

Language should remain welcoming, factual, inclusive, and respectful.

---

# Preferred Language

DiaperScout favours language that is:

- Curious
- Welcoming
- Precise
- Inclusive
- Factual
- Friendly
- Respectful

Language should encourage exploration and understanding rather than judgement or persuasion.

---

# Words We Prefer

| Preferred | Instead of |
|-----------|------------|
| Explore | Browse, Shop |
| Discover | Find |
| Guide | Catalogue |
| Product Entry | Listing |
| Community Observation | Review |
| Explorer | User (where appropriate) |
| Verified Data | Official Facts |
| Manufacturer | Company (when referring to the producer) |
| Collection | Inventory |
| Compare | Versus |

These are editorial preferences rather than absolute rules.

Consistency is more important than perfection.

---

# Words We Avoid

The following language should generally be avoided because it conflicts with the philosophy of DiaperScout.

## Commercial Language

Avoid:

- Buy now
- Lowest price
- Best deal
- Limited offer
- Bargain
- Must buy
- Hurry
- Don't miss out

These phrases encourage purchasing rather than discovery.

---

## Judgemental Language

Avoid:

- Normal
- Weird
- Strange
- Embarrassing
- Proper diaper
- Real user

These words make unnecessary value judgements about people or products.

---

## Absolute Claims

Avoid statements such as:

- Best diaper
- Perfect product
- Everyone agrees
- Guaranteed leakproof
- Ultimate protection
- The only choice

Unless objectively verifiable, these claims should not be presented as fact.

---

## Medical Assumptions

Avoid language that assumes why somebody uses a product.

Examples include:

- Patient
- Sufferer
- Medical user
- Healthy people
- Disabled people

Instead, describe situations rather than identities wherever possible.

---

# Tone Checklist

Before publishing copy, ask:

- Is it welcoming?
- Is it respectful?
- Is it accurate?
- Is it inclusive?
- Is it encouraging curiosity?
- Could someone feel judged by this wording?

If the answer to the final question is yes, reconsider the wording.

---

# Editorial Principles

When there is a choice between being clever and being clear, choose clarity.

When there is a choice between being persuasive and being informative, choose information.

When there is a choice between being sensational and being respectful, choose respect.

When there is a choice between simplifying and remaining accurate, choose accuracy without unnecessary complexity.

Every word should help someone understand the world of absorbent products a little better.
