# Canon

This document records the canonical decisions that define the DiaperScout universe.

Canon exists to ensure consistency across documentation, artwork, user experience, marketing, technical implementation, and community contributions.

Unless superseded by a later documented decision, the entries in this document represent the official position of the project.

---

# Philosophy

## Canon 001

DiaperScout is an explorer's guide to absorbent products.

It is not primarily:

- A shopping application
- A review website
- An affiliate platform
- A price comparison service

These features may exist, but they do not define the project.

Status: Accepted

---

## Canon 002

Discovery comes before commerce.

Helping people understand products is more important than helping people purchase them.

Commercial features must never compromise the educational purpose of the guide.

Status: Accepted

---

## Canon 003

Everyone is welcome.

DiaperScout welcomes all people with an interest in absorbent products.

No assumptions are made about why an Explorer uses or appreciates a product.

Status: Accepted

---

# Editorial

## Canon 004

Objective information should be clearly distinguished from community observations.

Verified data and personal experience are both valuable, but they serve different purposes.

Status: Accepted

---

## Canon 005

Respectful language is mandatory throughout the project.

Documentation, user interface text, marketing material, and community interaction should remain welcoming, factual, and inclusive.

Status: Accepted

---

## Canon 006

DiaperScout avoids sensationalism.

The project exists to inform, educate, and celebrate discovery.

It should never rely on clickbait, fear, embarrassment, or exaggerated claims.

Status: Accepted

---

# World

## Canon 007

Every absorbent product deserves documentation.

Products are not considered more or less worthy based on popularity, price, intended audience, or availability.

Status: Accepted

---

## Canon 008

The guide is never complete.

DiaperScout is a living project.

New discoveries, corrections, photographs, measurements, and historical information continually improve the guide.

Status: Accepted

---

## Canon 009

Curiosity is encouraged.

Exploration should always feel rewarding.

Users should be encouraged to discover products, compare information, and expand their understanding.

Status: Accepted

---

# Community

## Canon 010

Community contributions improve the guide.

Contributions should be encouraged while maintaining high standards for accuracy, civility, and evidence.

Status: Accepted

---

## Canon 011

Mistakes are expected.

Correcting information strengthens the guide.

Transparency is more valuable than pretending to be perfect.

Status: Accepted

---

# Design

## Canon 012

Every feature should reinforce the Explorer's Guide identity.

If a feature feels more like a shop than a guide, it should be reconsidered.

Status: Accepted

---

# Maintaining Canon

Canon should evolve deliberately.

Major philosophical or editorial decisions should be added to this document only after they have been discussed and accepted.

If a canonical decision changes, the previous entry should remain in version history through Git rather than being silently removed.

This allows the evolution of DiaperScout to be understood over time.
